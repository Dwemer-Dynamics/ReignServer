Create an exceptionally photorealistic scene inside a **Sturgian castle library and record chamber**, culturally grounded in the cold northern principalities and their mixture of fortified stone construction, heavy timber craftsmanship, trading traditions, clan authority, and preserved written records.

The chamber should feel warmer and more enclosed than its Vlandian or Imperial equivalent.

Lower stone walls support extensive dark timber construction. Thick carved wooden posts and ceiling beams dominate the room. Small shuttered windows admit cold blue northern daylight, while a large masonry hearth or enclosed brazier gives the interior warm amber illumination.

Heavy timber shelving contains a smaller but valuable collection of bound chronicles, imported books, parchment records, rolled maps, trade agreements, genealogies, household accounts, legal settlements, military reports, and records of tribute.

Wooden chests with iron fittings protect important documents from damp and fire.

A broad scarred table occupies the center, surrounded by sturdy benches and chairs. Fur or woven wool may cover some seats for warmth. Quills, parchment, ink, sealing wax, wooden tally pieces, rolled maps, knives used for trimming quills, and small document bundles show that the room remains actively used.

Sturgian activities should emphasize:

- Clan genealogy.
- Trade agreements.
- River and overland routes.
- Tribute records.
- Land disputes.
- Feuds and settlements.
- Military campaigns.
- Border reports.
- Household obligations.
- Old chronicles.
- Foreign correspondence.
- Studying imported books.
- Reviewing winter stores or trade accounts.
- Recording marriages and inheritance.
- Examining maps.
- Preserving accounts of important battles or rulers.

Decorative elements should include intricate northern wood carving, woven textiles, restrained geometric designs, iron fittings, animal-pattern ornament where culturally appropriate, and practical objects associated with a cold northern fortress.

Avoid turning the room into a Viking fantasy hall. This is distinctly **Sturgian**: a settled northern aristocratic culture connected to towns, rivers, trade, fortified estates, and clan politics.

Maximum photographic realism.
