Create an exceptionally photorealistic cinematic scene inside a **Nord throne room during quiet unofficial hours**, when no legal hearing, oath-taking, formal audience, military report, trade judgment, or ceremonial gathering is underway.

The chamber remains the formal seat of a settled northern maritime ruler, but without an assembled court it feels larger, darker, and more reflective.

Use:

- Massive rough-cut stone walls.
- Enormous dark oak beams.
- Heavy carved timber pillars.
- Narrow windows.
- Gray sea, harbor, snow, mountains, rain, or storm clouds visible where appropriate.
- Great hearths.
- Iron chandeliers.
- Heavy lanterns.
- Long woven banners.
- Knotwork.
- Wave patterns.
- Ship motifs.
- Shields.
- Ceremonial weapons.
- Sea charts.
- Harbor maps.
- Oath records.
- Written laws.
- Objects returned from distant voyages.

The throne should dominate the far end on a broad stone or timber dais.

Use massive carved oak, restrained interlace, wave or ship motifs, iron or bronze reinforcement, and heavy woven upholstery.

Do not use horned imagery, magical runes, skull thrones, or fantasy spectacle.

Outside formal hours, the room may contain:

- Sea charts left rolled.
- Legal books closed.
- Empty side benches.
- Oath documents stored safely.
- Ceremonial objects returned to stands.
- A few lamps burning.
- Firelight.
- Rain or wind audible beyond the windows.

- Looking at the throne.
- Sitting on the dais steps.
- Standing near the hearth.
- Watching a storm.
- Remembering something that happened here.
- Thinking about law.

- Quietly meeting another supplied character.
- Waiting for someone.
- Seeking solitude.
- Avoiding the Main Hall.
- Thinking about what kind of person should occupy such a throne.
- Wondering whether anyone actually deserves it.
