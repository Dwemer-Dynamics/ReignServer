Create an exceptionally photorealistic scene inside a **Sturgian castle portrait gallery and clan memorial corridor**.

Thick stone walls support dark timber beams and carved wooden uprights. Small windows admit cold northern daylight. Iron sconces and lamps provide warm amber light.

- Heavy carved timber frames.
- Painted family portraits.
- Clan symbols.
- Old shields.
- Swords.
- Axes used as ceremonial memorial objects.
- Trade objects inherited from distant journeys.
- Marriage gifts.
- Carved memorial plaques.
- Old banners.
- Written genealogies.
- Documents recording alliances or settlements.
- Objects associated with important ancestors.

The woodwork may contain restrained northern geometric carving and animal motifs.

Some portraits appear old, darkened, cracked, or repaired.

The room should communicate generations of **family memory and clan obligation**, rather than purely artistic collecting.

No invented humans may appear.

Any visible portrait must depict only a supplied source character, otherwise it must be covered, cropped, turned away, or too indistinct to contain an identifiable human.

Likely activities include:

- Studying clan ancestors.
- Examining a remembered warrior.
- Looking at a marriage connection.
- Thinking about an old feud.
- Reading a carved memorial.
- Comparing clan symbols.
- Examining an old weapon associated with an ancestor.
- Thinking about obligations inherited from the dead.
- Remembering a relative.
- Considering one's own place within the clan.
- Quietly meeting someone away from the hall.
- Reflecting on whether family stories are true.
- Looking at an ancestor who brought either honor or disgrace.
