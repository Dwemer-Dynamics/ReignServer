Character Memory

World memory is stored per campaign in PostgreSQL with full-text and semantic indexing. Recent NPC-specific conversations remain available as attributed raw turns; older continuity is supplied by scene summaries, middle-term consolidation, rolling arcs, and ranked retrieval. Complete group transcripts are retained when the NPC participated.

Beliefs, rumors, interpretations, and commitments also enter a time-versioned knowledge graph with source lineage, perspective ownership, validity intervals, and supersession links.

Recent Raw Continuity

Default: 30 attributed turns. Included group sessions are kept whole, so the actual count may exceed the target. The character ceiling is a prompt-safety limit; approximately four characters equal one token.
