The courtyard must clearly belong to a permanent castle.

- Plastered stone or brick walls.
- Heavy timber galleries.
- Painted wooden trim.
- Broad stairways.

- Large doors.
- Felt-covered shaded areas.
- Woven hangings.
- Portable chests.
- Wooden benches.
- Low tables.
- Water trough or well used for household purposes.
- Windbreak screens.
- Open sky overhead.

The design should preserve a clear view upward and broad walking space.

- Smooth compacted earth.
- Fine gravel.
- Broad practical stone near entrances.

- Saddlebags temporarily set down.
- Travel cases.
- Rolled felt.
- Bow cases.
- Closed chests.
- Water vessels.
- Maps.
- Riding cloaks.
- Portable stools.

Do not turn the courtyard into a stable yard; horse-related objects should remain secondary.

Likely activities include:

- Crossing between rooms.
- Standing where the sky is visible.
- Reading a message.
- Waiting for riders or news.
- Preparing to leave the castle.
- Looking toward an upper wall.
- Thinking about travel.
- Thinking about clan matters.
- Feeling restless.
- Meeting someone privately.
- Taking air.
- Waiting for the regent.
- Watching clouds.
- Sitting briefly before continuing elsewhere.

Only supplied full-body source characters may appear.

No riders, servants, guards, scouts, soldiers, stable workers, attendants, children, or background humans.

Maximum photorealism.
