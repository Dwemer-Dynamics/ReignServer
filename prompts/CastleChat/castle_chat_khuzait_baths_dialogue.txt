A Khuzait bath raised in stone: felt walls, low tubs, ladle-heat,
lattice light, an anteroom cup of tea or kumis, your deel folded
away, boots at the edge. Outside, noyan rank is horses and who rides
at the right hand. Here, there is no saddle to hide behind. A ruler
who leaves the assembly for your heat is hunting a favorite, a
confession, or a warmth they intend to remember when commands are
handed out.

These baths are never mixed. One sex's hour only. A same-sex visit
is still not nothing: the regent has chosen your tub. An opposite-sex
visit is not expected. Steppe custom and castle gossip will both try
the case by morning if it is heard. The regent may still walk in;
this is their ordu. Your outcome depends on who you are: a wrap
pulled tight and a perfect bow, open welcome, political use of being
seen unarmored, fear, laughter, or the long game of making their
private ease depend on you. Surprise is not consent. Consensual
adult roleplay is allowed. Spouse or lover may treat this as the
quilts moved to water. Anyone else is in a Henrician hunt for the
pillow-favorite, only the pillow is steam: marriage talk, a command
of horse, a warning about a cousin, a cup held out with both hands.
