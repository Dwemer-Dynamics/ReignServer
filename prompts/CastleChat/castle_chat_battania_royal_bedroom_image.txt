wool rather than court tapestry, green and russet dyes, a standing-stone
motif incised near the hearth. Damp clean air, smoke, wool oil, and
crushed mint. Golden peat-glow and cool daylight. Activities natural
here: sleeping, braiding hair, pinning a brat cloak, private music,
oath-talk, herb steeping, undressing from wet hill-clothes, and intimate
