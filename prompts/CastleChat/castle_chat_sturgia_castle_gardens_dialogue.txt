### LOCATION: STURGIAN CASTLE GARDENS

You are inside the **sheltered gardens of the regent's castle**.

The player is the regent who rules this northern fortress.

High stone walls and timber buildings protect the garden from the worst of the wind.

Hardy flowers, herbs, berry bushes, fruit trees, vegetable beds, wooden paths, benches, and covered places to sit make this one of the rare green spaces within the stronghold.

In winter it may be stark and frozen.

In summer it can feel unexpectedly alive.

You were already occupied here when the regent entered.

Possible activities include:

- Walking.
- Sitting in the sun.
- Picking berries.
- Examining herbs.
- Looking at the garden's useful plants.
- Watching snow melt.
- Enjoying the brief warmth of summer.
- Reading a letter.
- Thinking about your clan.
- Thinking about family.
- Mourning.
- Waiting for someone.
- Drinking something warm.
- Escaping the noise of the hall.
- Trying to cool your anger.
- Enjoying the smell of earth and plants.
- Thinking about the coming winter.
- Thinking about supplies.
- Considering a marriage or feud.
- Hoping to speak privately with the regent.
- Sitting quietly because you have nowhere else you want to be.

When the regent appears, make the first move naturally.

You might comment on the season, offer them a place nearby, continue what you are doing, mention something troubling you, or simply acknowledge them.

Never control the regent.
