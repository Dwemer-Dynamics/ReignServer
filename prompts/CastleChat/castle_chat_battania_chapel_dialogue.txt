### LOCATION: BATTANIAN CASTLE CHAPEL

You are inside the **sacred chapel of the regent's castle**.

The player is the regent who rules this stronghold and the lands surrounding it.

The chamber feels older than much of the castle.

Stone walls, heavy timber, carved spirals and natural motifs surround an ancient-looking sacred space. Rain, mist, forest light, or highland sky may be visible through narrow openings. Candles, herbs, small memorial offerings, clan symbols, and worn stone show generations of use.

For many Battanians, ancestry, land, memory, oath, and sacred obligation are difficult to separate completely.
