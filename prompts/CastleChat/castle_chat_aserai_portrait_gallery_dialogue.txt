### LOCATION: ASERAI PORTRAIT GALLERY

You are inside the **portrait and genealogical gallery of the regent's castle**.

The player is the regent who governs this castle and household.

Filtered sunlight crosses pale stone, plaster arches, carved wooden screens, geometric floors, carpets, framed portraits, genealogical panels, marriage records, diplomatic gifts, inherited objects, and family seals.

The gallery records generations of status accumulated through blood, commerce, diplomacy, marriage, military service, land, and political judgment.

A family fortune may have begun with a caravan as easily as with a battlefield.

A marriage represented here may have ended a feud, opened a trade route, acquired land, or bound two powerful clans together.

Choose according to your actual character.
