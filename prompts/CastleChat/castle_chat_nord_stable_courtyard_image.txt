Create an exceptionally photorealistic cinematic scene inside the **stable courtyard of a Nord castle**, distinctly reflecting a northern maritime aristocracy whose horses are important for inland travel, warfare, hunting, hauling, communication, and movement between coastal settlements even though the sea remains central to Nord identity.

This should not simply copy the Sturgian version.

The stable courtyard should feel influenced by **coastal weather, salt, rain, wind, steep terrain, and the need for durable working animals**.

- Massive rough-cut stone walls.
- Heavy dark oak stable buildings.
- Steep roofs.
- Wide eaves.
- Covered walkways.
- Iron-bound doors.
- Rain gutters.
- Drainage channels.
- Windbreaks.
- Hay lofts.
- Water troughs.
- Feed storage.
- Saddle racks.
- Bridles.
- Pack saddles.
- Hunting gear.
- Blankets.
- Saddlebags.
- Brushes.
- Hoof tools.
- Timber carts.
- Coiled ropes.
- Equipment for overland journeys to harbors or settlements.

Rain may make portions damp, but the surface must not become blocky or visually blotchy.

Horses should generally be hardy and practical:

- Riding horses.
- War horses.
- Hunting mounts.
- Pack horses.
- Strong working horses.

Avoid massive fantasy draft horses unless functionally appropriate.

Possible activities include:

- Grooming.
- Checking hooves.
- Cleaning mud.
- Saddling.
- Preparing a horse for an inland journey.
- Preparing pack equipment.
- Checking tack after rain.
- Feeding.
- Adjusting blankets.
- Inspecting a horse after hard travel.
- Preparing for a hunt.
- Waiting for weather to improve.
- Repairing straps.
- Standing beneath shelter while tending a horse.
- Preparing to travel between castle and harbor.
