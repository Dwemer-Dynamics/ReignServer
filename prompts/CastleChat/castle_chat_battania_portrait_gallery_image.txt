Create an exceptionally photorealistic cinematic scene inside a **Battanian castle portrait gallery and ancestral passage**, reflecting Battanian traditions of clan memory, oral history, land, kinship, heroic ancestry, woodland symbolism, and the preservation of family stories.

This gallery should feel older, more intimate, and more organic than the formal galleries of Vlandia or the Empire.

Rough but carefully fitted stone walls support enormous dark timber beams. The chamber may curve slightly with the older structure of the stronghold rather than forming a perfectly symmetrical palace corridor.

Tall narrow openings or windows reveal hints of forest, mist, hills, or rain outside.

Carved wooden frames use:

- Spirals.
- Knotwork.
- Interlocking patterns.
- Leaves.
- Stylized animals.
- Branch-like motifs.

Portraits are accompanied by:

- Clan emblems.
- Carved memorial tablets.
- Old weapons.
- Hunting trophies that do not depict human figures.
- Banners.
- Pieces of jewelry.
- Written genealogies.
- Recorded songs.
- Land maps.
- Tokens associated with marriages or oaths.
- Objects connected to specific family stories.

The gallery should feel like a place where **written history and remembered story overlap**.

Someone may know an ancestor from a painted portrait, while another person remembers a completely different version of that ancestor through stories told beside fires.

Only supplied source characters may be represented as human beings.

No invented ancestral faces or bodies may appear in paintings.

Portraits containing unsupplied ancestors must be covered, angled, cropped, obscured, or unreadable.

Likely activities include:

- Studying ancestors.
- Remembering family stories.
- Comparing written inscriptions with oral tradition.
- Looking at a clan founder.
- Thinking about land inherited through generations.
- Examining an old marriage connection.
- Remembering a famous hunt or battle.
- Reflecting on family rivalries.
- Quietly speaking to someone in a secluded portion of the gallery.
- Considering whether the current generation honors its ancestors.
- Questioning whether an old hero deserved their reputation.

### CHARACTER REQUIREMENTS

Every visible human must correspond to a supplied full-body source image.

No guards, servants, ancestors, druids, courtiers, children, reflected people, silhouettes, or artistic human figures unless they use a supplied identity.

Maintain exact facial identity, age, skin, hair, scars, height, body mass, proportions, musculature, and physique.

Clothing may change to authentic Battanian noble or civilian clothing.

Avoid generic Celtic fantasy styling.

Maximum photographic realism.
