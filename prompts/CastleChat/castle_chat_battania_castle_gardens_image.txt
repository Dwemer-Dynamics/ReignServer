Create an exceptionally photorealistic cinematic scene inside the **gardens of a Battanian highland castle**, reflecting a culture closely tied to forests, hills, medicinal plants, hunting, clan land, old stonework, natural beauty, and a less rigid distinction between cultivated garden and surrounding landscape.

The garden should **not** resemble a perfectly formal court garden.

It should feel as though a section of the highland environment has been deliberately protected and shaped without stripping away its natural character.

- Heather.

- Medicinal herbs.
- Berry bushes.
- Fruit trees.
- Hazel.

- Ivy.
- Woodland plants.
- Mushrooms where naturally appropriate.
- Small clearings.
- Herb beds worked into the natural terrain.
- Running water.

- Timber footbridge.
- Carved wooden benches.

Castle walls, towers, and timber buildings should remain visible through the greenery.

Mist, rain, damp soil, shifting woodland light, and the sound or appearance of water should contribute strongly to the atmosphere.

Possible activities include:

- Walking winding paths.
- Gathering herbs.
- Examining medicinal plants.
- Sitting beneath a tree.

- Waiting for someone.
- Practicing quiet woodcraft.
- Looking beyond the castle toward forested hills.
- Seeking solitude in a place that feels less confined.

### CHARACTER REQUIREMENTS

Only supplied full-body source characters may appear.

No gardeners, druids, servants, hunters, guards, children, or background figures.

Preserve exact face, apparent age, natural skin, hair, scars, height, build, body mass, muscularity, and complete physique.

Clothing may change to realistic Battanian outdoor civilian or noble garments using wool, linen, leather, woven fabrics, cloaks, boots, dresses, tunics, trousers, and culturally appropriate patterns.

Maximum photographic realism.
