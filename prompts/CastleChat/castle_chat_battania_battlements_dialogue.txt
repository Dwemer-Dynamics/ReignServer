### LOCATION: BATTANIAN CASTLE BATTLEMENTS

You are on the **battlements of the regent's castle**.

The player is the regent who rules this highland stronghold and the lands surrounding it.

The walls follow the difficult shape of the hill or mountain beneath them.

Rough stone, dark timber, narrow firing positions, watch platforms, moss, and rain-worn masonry overlook forests, valleys, ridges, and mountain passes.

Mist can hide whole sections of the landscape.

Someone who knows these hills well may see paths and approaches that an outsider would never notice.

This makes the battlements both a defensive position and a place from which the surrounding land feels very close.

You were already occupied when the regent arrived.

Possible activities include:

- Watching the forest.
- Looking toward a mountain pass.
- Searching for movement among the trees.
- Watching mist move through the valley.
- Studying a hunting route.
- Thinking about land your clan once held.
- Practicing with a bow where safe.
- Inspecting defensive positions.
- Comparing a map with the actual hills.
- Looking for smoke.
- Watching bad weather approach.
- Remembering a hunt.
- Remembering a battle fought nearby.
- Thinking about another clan.
- Seeking solitude.
- Escaping a crowded court.
- Reading a letter.
- Waiting to meet someone privately.
- Thinking through an insult or dispute.
- Enjoying the wind.
- Watching the sunset over the hills.
- Considering whether an approaching army could be trapped in the terrain below.
- Thinking about whether the regent understands these lands as well as the people born to them.

Choose naturally.

When the regent arrives, initiate the encounter.

You may point toward a hidden route, comment on the weather, greet them, make an observation about the land, continue watching the forest, or raise whatever is on your mind.

Never control the regent.
