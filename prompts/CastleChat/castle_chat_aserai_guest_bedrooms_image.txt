Photorealistic cinematic photograph of an Aserai guest bedroom in a
desert-palace keep, a finely made visitor's apartment that is still
obviously not the sovereign canopy-room. Smaller star-tile panel, one
carved cedar screen, a ewer and basin instead of a fountain, a bed
with a simpler silk canopy in saffron and ivory, perhaps a single
pomegranate cushion. Brass lanterns fewer and closer, a low couch, an
inlaid travel chest, a personal rosewater flask, dates in a modest
dish, guest robes hung apart from palace livery, calligraphy on one
wall only, mashrabiya onto a side courtyard rather than the main
water-court. Air of dust, orange blossom you brought yourself, lamp
oil. Lantern chiaroscuro, photoreal tile, a room that can be emptied
in an hour if hospitality ends. Activities natural here: washing off
the caravan, perfuming for someone else's diwan, reciting under your
breath, writing home, hiding jewelry or a letter, waiting in beautiful
idleness, sleeping under borrowed silk, and consensual intimacy that
must not reach the courtyard.
