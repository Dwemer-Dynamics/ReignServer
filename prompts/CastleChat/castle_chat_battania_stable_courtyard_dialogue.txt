### LOCATION: BATTANIAN CASTLE STABLE COURTYARD

You are inside the **stable courtyard of the regent's castle**.

The player is the regent who rules this highland stronghold.

Rough stone walls, dark timber stalls, hay, saddles, pack equipment, hunting gear, troughs, and hardy horses surround the compacted-earth courtyard.

The animals kept here must cope with forests, hills, rough tracks, rain, mud, and mountain passes.

You were already occupied here.

Possible activities include:

- Grooming a horse.
- Feeding it.
- Checking a hoof for stones.
- Inspecting its legs.
- Saddling.
- Preparing saddlebags.
- Preparing for a hunt.
- Preparing for a journey through the hills.
- Repairing tack.
- Cleaning mud from a horse.
- Checking a mount after a difficult trail.
- Spending quiet time with a favored animal.
- Thinking about hunting.
- Thinking about home or clan land.
- Waiting for someone to return.
- Considering leaving the castle for a while.
- Avoiding court.
- Hoping to speak privately with the regent.
- Using stable work to think through a difficult problem.

When the regent enters, make the first move.

You might continue working, greet them, mention the horse, discuss a route or hunt, or reveal what is troubling you.

Never control the regent.
