Create an exceptionally photorealistic cinematic scene inside an **Imperial throne room during quiet hours when no court session, legal hearing, petitioning, or formal ceremony is taking place**.

The chamber remains a powerful symbol of Imperial state authority, but its bureaucratic and ceremonial machinery is temporarily dormant.

Use:

- Monumental fitted stone and brick.
- Broad rounded arches.
- Marble or limestone columns.
- Worn geometric mosaics.
- Painted plaster.
- Bronze lamps.
- High windows.
- Heavy formal doors.
- Imperial geometric ornament.
- Non-human state symbolism.
- Military standards.
- Administrative insignia.
- Maps.
- Legal tablets.
- Empty official seating.

The ruling seat stands upon a broad raised dais.

It should be imposing, symmetrical, and institutionally formal rather than fantastical.

No active officials or court personnel should be present unless supplied as source characters.

The unused room may contain:

- Closed legal document boxes.
- Rolled maps.
- Wax tablets stacked away.
- Ceremonial staffs returned to stands.
- Empty seats.
- Extinguished lamps.
- An unattended writing table.
- Old inscriptions.
- Historical plaques.
- Military standards.

Unofficial activities may include:

- Studying the architecture.
- Reading old inscriptions.
- Looking at the ruling seat.
- Examining historical standards.
- Reading a private letter.
- Reviewing a document before tomorrow's proceedings.
- Rehearsing rhetoric.
- Practicing a speech.
- Thinking about legitimacy.
- Thinking about history.
- Comparing the current government with former rulers.
- Debating politics quietly with another supplied character.
- Sitting somewhere usually reserved for officials.
- Enjoying the acoustics of the empty chamber.
- Seeking solitude.
- Reflecting upon an earlier proceeding.
- Walking through the room between other parts of the palace.

### CHARACTER REQUIREMENTS

Only supplied full-body source characters may appear as human beings.

No officials, guards, scribes, senators, servants, attendants, petitioners, soldiers, or background people.

No invented humans in mosaics, reliefs, statues, or paintings.

Preserve exact source identity and complete physique.

Clothing may change to realistic Imperial noble, civilian, administrative, or military clothing appropriate to the individual.

Avoid theatrical Roman costume.

Maximum live-action photorealism.
