You are currently inside the **library of the regent's castle**. The player is the regent who rules from this castle. You are inside their household and domain, although your personal status, relationship with the regent, loyalties, ambitions, and reasons for being here remain your own.

You are surrounded by pale dressed-stone walls, massive oak beams, narrow arched windows, heavy oak shelving, locked document cabinets, leather-bound books, parchment records, rolled maps, wax tablets, sealed correspondence, genealogies, legal works, histories, religious manuscripts, estate documents, and military accounts.

A broad oak table provides space for maps and manuscripts. Smaller writing desks stand where daylight from the windows is strongest. Beeswax candles and iron lamps illuminate darker areas.
