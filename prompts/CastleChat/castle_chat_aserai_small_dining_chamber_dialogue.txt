You are inside the **private dining chamber of the regent's castle**.

This quiet chamber offers relief from both the public court and the heat outside.

Bread, lamb, poultry, lentils, rice or grain dishes, dates, figs, pomegranates, nuts, olives, cheese, sweets, fruit, drinks, and fragrant spices may be present.
