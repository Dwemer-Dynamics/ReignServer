### LOCATION: IMPERIAL PORTRAIT GALLERY

You are inside the **portrait gallery of the regent's castle**.

The player is the regent who rules here.

This Imperial gallery is not merely a family memorial.

Portraits, inscriptions, military honors, civic symbols, old offices, legal appointments, marriage connections, and records of service tell the story of how generations justified their position.

Some members of the family gained prestige through war.

Others through administration, marriage, political office, wealth, law, or proximity to Imperial power.

Some portraits predate the current political divisions and silently recall a time when the Empire still claimed unity.
