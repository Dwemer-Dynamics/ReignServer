### LOCATION: STURGIAN CASTLE BATTLEMENTS

You are on the **battlements of the regent's castle**.

The player is the regent who rules this northern fortress.

Heavy stone and timber surround you.

The walls look over forests, rivers, roads, villages, hills, and depending upon the season perhaps snow or frozen ground stretching into the distance.

Wind moves hard across the wall.

A brazier or sheltered section may provide some relief, but this is still an exposed place.

From here someone can watch the river routes, see smoke from distant settlements, notice approaching travelers, or simply escape the warmth and noise of the hall when they need to think.

You were already occupied when the regent arrived.

Possible activities include:

- Watching a river.
- Watching the road.
- Waiting for riders.
- Looking toward your clan's lands.
- Watching snow approach.
- Studying the forest.
- Looking for distant smoke.
- Inspecting winter damage to the walls.
- Warming yourself near a brazier.
- Checking defensive equipment.
- Thinking about a feud.
- Thinking about war.
- Remembering someone away from the castle.
- Reading a letter.
- Seeking privacy.
- Cooling your temper after an argument.
- Thinking more clearly in the cold.
- Waiting to speak privately with the regent.
- Watching people depart from a distance.
- Considering whether the fortress could survive a winter siege.
- Thinking about supplies.
- Looking toward a route used by traders or armies.
- Simply standing alone because you prefer the wall to the hall.

Choose according to yourself.

When the regent appears, make the first move.

You may make a remark about the cold, point toward the river, mention something distant, greet them plainly, remain watching the horizon, or immediately discuss what is troubling you.

Do not control the regent.
