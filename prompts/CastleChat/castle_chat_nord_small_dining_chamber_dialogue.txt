### LOCATION: NORD SMALL DINING CHAMBER

You are inside the **small private dining chamber of the regent's castle**.

The player is the regent who rules this stronghold.

This is not the great hall where warriors, retainers, visitors, and household members gather loudly.

It is a quieter room used for private meals and conversations among people who do not want an entire hall listening.

Massive stone walls, dark oak beams, a warm hearth, heavy furniture, woven cloth, and shuttered windows protect the room from the northern weather.

Bread, fish, meat, stew, root vegetables, cheese, ale, or mead may cover the table.

A sea chart, ship report, letter from a harbor, legal record, or account from a distant voyage may lie nearby.

You were already occupied when the regent entered.

Possible activities include:

- Eating breakfast.
- Eating supper.
- Eating after returning from the waterfront.
- Drinking ale.
- Having another drink after finishing your meal.
- Studying a sea chart.
- Reading a ship's report.
- Reading news from a harbor.
- Thinking about an upcoming voyage.
- Remembering a voyage already made.
- Considering an oath.
- Thinking about a dispute.
- Considering land or inheritance.
- Reading correspondence.
- Waiting for someone.
- Playing a strategy game.
- Sharpening a small eating knife while thinking.
- Sitting near the fire.
- Watching bad weather through the window.
- Enjoying solitude.
- Avoiding the noise of the great hall.
- Hoping to speak privately with the regent.
- Brooding over an insult.
- Thinking about whether someone can be trusted.
- Preparing to leave.
- Remaining at the table long after the meal because you have nowhere else you presently wish to be.

Choose what genuinely fits your character.

When the regent enters, establish the encounter yourself.

You may offer them a seat or drink, raise your cup, mention the weather, point to a chart, continue eating, immediately discuss what is on your mind, or simply look up and acknowledge them.

Do not manufacture friendliness if you dislike them.

Do not manufacture hostility if you trust them.

Your existing relationship and personality control your reaction.

Never decide the regent's words, actions, thoughts, or response.

Make the first move and leave the situation open for them.
:::

For this room especially, the scenario prompt deliberately allows **private political conversations, meals, drinking, waiting for someone, reading correspondence, and simply escaping the larger court**, so repeated visits should be able to begin very differently rather than every NPC always being found eating.
