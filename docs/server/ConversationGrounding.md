# Conversation originals and grounding

The author-only original is the authority for player history. Complete player dialogue and narration reach the writer in their original order, subject only to explicit speech audiences such as whispers. Inferred thoughts, unfamiliar physical verbs and unresolved classifier output never remove player story text. NPC-authored private narration retains its observer boundary. Neither model interpretation nor scene narration authorizes native game effects.

Group floor planning admits the same original without calling the interpreter. Its provisional observer row is retired atomically when the accepted audience projection arrives; its text remains auditable and it cannot compete with the active source in recall. Repeated floor requests cannot resurrect it. Eligible player rows retain their complete saved narration through memory routing, topic indexing, late-interaction views, handoff and commitment extraction, including legacy rows without interpretation metadata. Source-only originals remain author-only and unindexed; audience, timeline, acceptance and as-of checks still apply. The `player-narrative-v3` index policy rebuilds older cached views through ordinary bounded maintenance without changing original turns or worker wire schemas. It cannot reconstruct wording already absent from an old accepted row.

`conversation_input_receipts` binds a contribution to its exact UTF-8 SHA-256, source-only turn, player, timeline and restore generation. Admission commits before interpretation/generation. Accepted response recovery is committed alongside accepted sources; the complete delivery receipt follows action publication. A recovered reply explicitly reports when later native publication is unconfirmed. Stable contribution IDs reject altered retries, share interpretation across observers, and never silently replay into another save generation. Whole-schema Save Sync snapshots include receipts and originals.

Originals use distinct negative ordering slots under the existing conversation publication lock. The table's unique session/order constraint previously collided on the constant `-1` and `INSERT OR IGNORE` silently dropped later originals. Sequential and concurrent admission now retain every original, retry by source identity, and reject conflicting content rather than ignoring an insert failure. Observable player/NPC turns keep their separate nonnegative order.

The Windows client journals exact text before supported spoken composers clear, flushing to a unique temporary file and atomically replacing the record. The journal lives at `%LOCALAPPDATA%/Reign/ConversationInputs`, partitioned by hashed campaign/timeline/player and contribution. It retains originals after acknowledgment. It does not automatically resend records across saves. A persistence exception prevents clearing the input; failed server requests can show locally pending lines. User-readable history uses source references; `sourceIntegrity` distinguishes verified original, missing original and unverified legacy display text.

Narrated contributions may use one bounded classifier call, cached durably for all listeners, to annotate scene proposals. The interpreter retains its legacy span annotations for diagnostics; these do not gate narrative delivery or require a reaction to each action. Bounds are 32,768 input characters, 64 narration blocks and 4,096 output tokens; oversized/malformed/ambiguous classification reports unresolved while complete player text remains available. The player observer projection uses `reign-observer-projection-v3` with complete source coverage. `narrativeInputIntegrity` records original/writer hashes, explicit audience scoping, and disabled thought filtering and reaction-evidence requirements. Whisper recipients receive the full contribution; bystanders retain surrounding physical narration while private speech is withheld. There is no inferred-thought or new private-thought syntax.

The writer receives protected current actions, narrative venue/proposals and a fresh independent native party roster. Native room defaults do not replace an accepted narrative venue. Resolved participant state marks `hasNarrativeLocation` only when an accepted location override exists: native/home fallbacks inherit the shared venue, independently accepted locations remain distinct, and a clothing-only revision cannot change location authority. Without a shared venue, remote participants retain their own locations. Opening establishment does not depend on nonempty writer scene metadata. Movement affecting an NPC requires its own accepted/refused decision and visible support. Narrative location persists through the scene handoff while clothing keeps hourly expiry. A new native anchor or timeline invalidates the old location. Current membership, role, town presence, witness access and historical travel are separate claims.

A present arrival into a named interlocutor's own venue establishes both the player and present host there; it does not ask the host to travel. A narrow source-bound arrival guard covers common physical venues and a missing possessive apostrophe even if classifier scene metadata is empty. Other vocabulary still uses the interpreter. Past/planned/imagined/negated arrivals, empty venues and absent owners do not establish a shared scene. The current venue controls date-free headings as well as dated headings; hall drift is checked in both the heading and physical narration.

Per-action `actionResponses`/`replyQuote` checks and their repairs are removed. The writer responds naturally to complete story context; a missing lexical acknowledgment or punctuation mismatch alone cannot fail a reply. Bounded repair still checks actual scene/body contradictions, supported roster/role claims, speaker attribution and existing mechanical/formal-action boundaries, including drinking. These checks are conservative deterministic coverage, not a complete natural-language proof. Prose repair retains an existing `actionGate` when the provider omits it; independently validated decision metadata and the remaining checks still control acceptance. Empty or malformed provider output still uses bounded format recovery and can fail.

The final accepted text is checked again after rewrites. `conversationGrounding` records final text hash, source ID, issues and checked/unresolved status. Successful repair uses `..`; unresolved usable dialogue uses `.,`. Structured repair status controls acceptance. Unresolved NPC speech stays attributed and unverified on recall; its proposed scene, relationship, gossip, identity and derived memory effects are withheld, including social-event joins and aggregate relationship effects. A separately authorized player action remains governed by its own transaction.

Party checks also bind a bare name to its immediately following personal description within the same paragraph. They do not carry that identity across paragraphs or attach another named person's sentence. Scene checks inspect each original narration block separately from adjacent speech, including current physical contact with venue walls and ambient descriptions, and explicit present-tense statements about being there. `*She enters the tavern.* I accept` cannot evade the venue check through its closing star or following speech. A repaired heading alone cannot certify a still-contradictory body. Historical, negated and nearby-venue mentions remain controls. An `hours ago` callback remains historical across an em dash, while a separate current-location statement still fails. Grounded headings use a supplied date and known settlement and resolve `her/his/their` venue ownership to the interlocutor; missing dates are not invented.

Attribution checks recognize bare `You asked what ...` claims and source questions introduced by `The question is ...`. Only exclusive source ownership supports a local pronoun correction; recipient inversions and ambiguous wording retain the bounded repair/failure path. Narrated actors remain bound to their source speaker rather than the current respondent.

The prepared state retains the shared starting venue before a proposed move. Refusal resolves back to that venue when the NPC has no independent accepted location, rather than its remote native/home fallback. Establishment displays the source-bound new venue for its actual subjects; moving one participant does not relocate others by display fallback.

## Historical audit and reviewed recovery

Use the existing `scripts/Repair-ConversationContinuity.ps1` with an exact validated artifact/hash. `-Mode Stage` accepts `reign-conversation-original-audit-v1`, exact `campaignId`, `timelineId`, and optional `reviewedCaptures`. It reads every original player turn across NPCs, constrains timeline/verified restored ancestry, and reports original availability and provenance gaps. It never calls a provider or changes the campaign. The result includes a normal `reign-continuity-repair-v1` manifest.

A reviewed capture must bind `turnId`, `campaignId`, `timelineId`, `sessionId`, `playerId`, exact `originalText`/`sourceHash`, original diagnostic `traceId` and `captureFileSha256`. The operator must inspect the retained diagnostic bytes and matching request identities; a hash-shaped string alone does not prove authenticity. Captures may not come from summaries, generated paraphrases, model responses or guessed wording. Unavailable originals remain explicitly unrecoverable.

`restore_original_input` adds an author-only original with a reverse link to the accepted turn and its complete source hash. It never edits the accepted turn's metadata or text, preserving the full-row proof used by restored memory. A capture retains its original timeline; repair targets the active timeline and requires verified Save Sync inheritance for an older source. History resolves eligible reverse links even outside its page window. Conflicting originals or changed accepted sources fail closed. It never changes NPC replies, knowledge, native actions or relationship scores. Preview rolls back; apply requires a reviewed manifest hash, verified database backup, closed game and stopped managed server. Manifest replay is idempotent. A subsequent managed checkpoint must retain the reviewed recovery before loading an older save; an intentional rollback still restores that save's exact history. Reprojecting historical NPC knowledge is a separate reviewed operation.

## Proof and limits

Use canonical validation and artifact-bound `prompt_efficiency` (`input_grounding_*`), `interaction_architecture`, `memory_precision`, relationship and Save Sync contracts. The shared atomic publisher has actual filesystem fault-injection tests. Fixtures cover the reported grass duel/massage, mixed private clauses, uncommon verbs, exact Unicode/whitespace, duplicate IDs, accepted-before-delivery crash recovery, restore rejection, scene persistence/refusal/native change, historical preview and original recovery. Provider and native acceptance remain separate: record actual interpreter output, final serialized writer input, response, receipt and reopened history on a disposable campaign. Private replay captures stay local and are not published with this public server source.
