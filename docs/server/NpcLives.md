# NPC Lives testing boundaries

Background Approach admission (2026-10-13): the worker outlives its initiating HTTP request, so it acquires ordinary campaign request admission before dialogue preparation, then verifies the saved generation and exact pending operation/session/day. This protects character profile/construction work as well as final publication during Save Sync replacement. Existing provider-wait replacement handling remains active; stale work stops before preparing context. The isolated admission regression verifies no interactive/provider work starts and the lease is released. This adds no campaign or provider authority.

Accurate Approach (2026-10-13): accuracy takes priority over latency. NPC Lives openings use ordinary dialogue character/context/memory/identity/relationship/scene preparation, the full output schema, capacity/source preflight and bounded grounding/repair stages. The client captures the same native profile, identity, presence, party roster, calendar, pregnancy, political and context-bundle evidence as conversation; group approaches carry their actual room and audience. An engine arrival cue is never player speech or proof of delivery, consent, a completed quest or a reward. Recognition and an expected return, letter or unfinished promise shape the reaction. Warmth and availability remain independent; no affinity threshold forces friendliness or romance. Prefer a brief gesture and one or two sentences while preserving required context and uncertainty. The configured Approach provider/model remains in use, including repairs, with the ordinary schema output allowance and the user's reasoning preference.

The ordinary dialogue path returns a draft before transcript/effect acceptance. Only a server-owned receipt bound to campaign, timeline, NPC, player, operation, generation, session, day and exact reply/activity can publish through NPC Lives. Recheck the actual session audience at publication. A malformed, interrupted, technical or unresolved draft records a retryable error with no invented activity or accepted conversation, no clock advance and no announcement acknowledgement. A valid retry retains once-only and silent-resume rules. In-flight results remain generation-bound. Provider calls, native acceptance, campaign advancement and deployment retain separate authorization gates.

The artifact-bound npc_lives suite adds production dialogue-envelope checks with the Ingunde expected-return/supply/letter/revised-date fixture, complete character state and prior exchanges, audience/affinity contexts, ordinary output/reasoning policy, server-only publication/tamper/rewind fences and rejected/finally accepted retry behavior. Existing observer, original-input, scene, motive, integrity and continuity contracts still apply; prompt_efficiency supplies broader shared-pipeline proof. Provider-free evidence establishes context delivery and deterministic rejection/publication, not arbitrary model accuracy. After deployment, inspect a real expected-return opening, positive/neutral/hostile familiarity, busy-but-welcoming behavior, unknown delivery outcomes, public privacy, one-hour resumption and close/reopen without a duplicate request. No new testing command, provider permission or campaign authority is added.


Provider-paced Approach (2026-10-12): the Approach-specific 2.5-second cutoff and Diagnostics timeout control are retired. A still-running provider cannot be replaced by a generic fallback due to elapsed time, including an expired deadline retained in an old save. Normal transport/provider errors and interrupted-job recovery retain their existing handling. Individual and party chats asynchronously poll the same saved operation until it completes or their screen closes; there is no eight-second polling cap. Closing stops UI polling without creating another provider request or discarding its durable result. Existing wilderness/social/event openings are unchanged. Isolated production tests verify an expired legacy deadline stays pending and a deliberately slow provider reply publishes exactly once after the retired cutoff. Native wait/close/reopen acceptance follows the combined deployment.

Initial Approach switch (2026-10-12): Options > NPC Lives and Storylines > **NPC speaks first when approached** controls only the added NPC Lives automatic Approach opening. It defaults on, is saved independently of stories and announcements, and preserves the subordinate activity-call preference while off. Existing wilderness/social/event setup and explicit NPC invitation openings use their existing routes and remain unchanged. Disabling while an Approach draft is in flight discards that unpublished draft without accepting a turn, moving the conversation clock, or acknowledging pending announcements; the player can speak normally and pending subjects remain available to their next accepted reply. The isolated endpoint/publication contracts and three-width Options browser fixture cover this boundary; native acceptance awaits deployment.

Summons entry safety (2026-10-12): explicit client startup installs and verifies 9 owned Harmony hooks for meeting stays, party spawning and five native settlement-entry economic handlers. Synchronous relocation uses a thread-local, exact-party scope to skip entry recruitment, prisoner selling, food/horse trading and garrison transfers while preserving other presence/quest events. The scope always ends on exit or exception; ordinary later hourly commerce is unaffected. Native `snapshot` adds read-only `meetingInterception` readiness/required/installed counts and bounded startup error plus `relocationCommerceSuppressed` (normally false outside relocation). Missing hooks hold new summons/relocation safely. These observations retain every existing disposable Current gate and grant no new mutation or consent authority. Reproduce and retain a failed before/after digest, deploy the repair, then retest genuine consent, actual due-day relocation, party contents and final duty restoration.

Server-captured review window (2026-10-12): ordinary prompt construction stores the exact pending heard announcement IDs in weak request-local server state, scoped to campaign, timeline, player, NPC and native day. The provider returns `announcementReview:{reviewedAll:true,disposition:"commented"|"no_comment",evidenceQuote:"..."}`. Published, nontechnical replies acknowledge only those captured IDs; later arrivals stay unread. Incoming payload fields cannot supply this authority. Approach captures the window through ordinary dialogue assembly and binds it to the server-owned validated draft for publication; incoming data cannot restore that authority. Empty/fallback replies, foreign scope and rewind cannot consume it. Legacy exact `throughId` and per-announcement replies remain compatible; there is no approximate ID matching. The isolated production tests cover 13 pending subjects, an arrival during generation, caller forgery, optional silence, replay and persistence. Native quality remains a separate check.

Canonical story continuity (2026-10-12): saved supporting bindings and the fixed destination govern the active purpose even when earlier dialogue contains different invented details. `NpcStoryInstance.LocationName` is captured from the same native roster as its bound ID; evaluation upgrades missing labels without rebinding or granting progress. Missing names are deferred. The existing reveal phase still hides future supporting identities. Verify a previously contradictory real conversation after deployment; prompt contracts alone do not prove the provider follows them.

Current-purpose instruction priority (2026-10-13): native testing found a reply repeating an older Zeonica tavern despite the correctly supplied Llanoc Hen binding. Prompt construction now captures the owned current story or leave purpose in weak request-local server state and supplies it as a separate system instruction in dialogue and event requests. It excludes other owners, unrelated active destinations, announcements and unreached identity reveals; incoming fields cannot forge it. The protected owner memory record remains available as context. Explicit summons instructions also distinguish `solo_camp` from historical `legacy_party` attendance. Retest the contradictory native conversation after deployment; these prompt contracts do not establish provider compliance.

Pending invitation continuity (2026-10-14): only accepted active stories can supply the separate current-purpose system instruction. An unaccepted invitation remains optional owner-only reference, retaining its exact identity, revision, proposal, destination and revealed bindings. Ordinary chat receives no authored activity script or progress objectives; an explicit review of that exact `npcStoryId` includes the activity as quoted offer data. Pending status never means a topic is new or that an earlier discussion did not happen: acknowledge supplied accepted history and answer the current contribution without replaying the setup or soliciting an opinion already given. Discussion grants no mechanical acceptance, progress, affinity or XP. The isolated `npc_lives` suite covers the Corein music/history regression in dialogue and social-event envelopes, exact/foreign offer review, reload, unchanged consent state and retained active-story authority. Real provider interpretation remains a native acceptance check after deployment.

First-party story definitions are version 2. Conversation objectives declare `CompletionOutcomes`; two substantive player contributions alone cannot finish a phase. A final `npcStoryAssessments` result must quote the player's accepted contribution and one complete, self-contained paragraph of the same committed NPC reply as `resolutionQuote`. Future plans, conditional willingness, cherry-picked fragments and fabricated or foreign quotes do not establish a conclusion. These are rejection guards, never keyword triggers for native actions. Existing active definitions stay pinned to their accepted version.

`NpcLifeFact.OutcomeObjectiveId` and `RequiredNarrativeOutcome` optionally condition a fact on a declared conversation outcome in the same phase. The rabbit outing supports `adopted` and `declined`; both finish a meaningful experience, but only `adopted` grants a pet. A conditional "if it passes" cannot grant the rabbit. Native travel, duels, rescues and inventory still require native receipts. See production NPC Lives self-tests and client MCP contracts for accepted-source/whole-reply binding, uncertainty, both choices, reload and reward replay coverage. New definitions should distinguish progress from an accomplished personal conclusion explicitly.

Native hideout mission initialization is asynchronous. The client binds the actual hideout MapEvent immediately and installs its companion witness through ordinary mission initialization. Witnessing requires the exact current mission, matching native battle, active story encounter and actual active companion agent. A deferred mission does not itself count as withdrawal. Completion still requires the native hideout callback and bound rescue/item evidence; launch failures retain their base error in the encounter record.

The native acceptance procedure is maintained in the paired client's `docs/agent/testing/FEATURE_HARNESSES.md`, and `reign.testing.json` owns its capabilities.

Bound destination setup is a separate operator fixture on the enrolled disposable Current. It accepts only an already accepted story identity and initializes that story's native settlement encounter, with no caller destination or outcome. Actual co-presence can then produce ordinary arrival evidence. Its durable movement journal prevents replay, including after an uncertain attempt; it is not evidence of natural travel or consent.

Invitation setup requires verification control, an explicitly armed enrolled disposable Current, retained recovery checkpoint, exact campaign/timeline/game instance, paused native time and an executing unexpired command lease. Story and leave setup create unaccepted authored requests only. Leave uses captured native personality and affinity, a feasible destination and a deterministic 3–7 day absence. Caller-supplied consent, personality, departure, progress and rewards are not accepted. The protected baseline is never changed.

Controlled encounters launch an already accepted story through its production adapter. Both real active mission agents and the exact acknowledged encounter/revision are required; hideouts also require the real battle and companion witness. A bounded native outcome drives ordinary completion callbacks. It does not fabricate travel, rescue, item possession, earlier chapters or consent, and cannot advance time or save. Command success is insufficient: inspect settled production evidence, once-only affinity/XP, failures and persistence separately.

Story duel proposals belong to the existing personal invitation and Story Location. A duplicate standalone `duel_player` proposal in that pending story context is refused as a whole; acceptance of other valid agreements and unrelated actions retains the normal signed protocol. Native co-presence is observed even while campaign time is paused.
